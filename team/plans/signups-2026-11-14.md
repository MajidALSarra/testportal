# Sign-ups and subscriptions: fix plan, 14 Nov 2026

Asked by Majid in #folderbird-agents (thread 1794599751.075549, 09:45 Riyadh): "do whatever it takes to increase signups and subscriptions, no matter the campaign cost, but don't compromise my country criteria."

Made by a five-stage review of web `origin/main` (675be73) and folderbird-agent 1.2.8.1 (1f14100): 51 findings, each checked in the source by two independent reviewers (3 dropped). The Coordinator then checked the live site.

## Corrections after checking the live site (14 Nov, 11:21 Riyadh, from Majid's laptop)

- **Production is NOT the old 14 Oct hotfix build.** The live /Download already has the "Create one free" link, and the last production deploy was 12 Nov 00:13 UTC. So the plan's "Before anyone starts" check is answered: fixes go on `main`.
- **`GoogleAds__ConfirmSignupLabel` IS set on production** (sticky slot setting; the name was read, never the value). So Step 0 is not "set the label". It is: prove the conversion fires with one real sign-up (accept cookies, register, open the email link in the same browser). Google shows it "unverified" because no one has confirmed through it yet.
- The live home page still has "Start on the Download page", no "Create your free account", and shows `LAPTOP-MOGV95CM` in the hero picture. Fix 1 applies as written.

## Two findings from Falcon's server logs that the review did not cover

- **3 in 4 people who download the installer never start the app.** Last 30 days: 36 browser downloads, 9 app starts within a day, 3 sign-in attempts. The likely wall is Windows' blue "Windows protected your PC" box (SmartScreen), where "Run anyway" hides behind "More info". The installer is EV-signed, but since 2024 an EV certificate no longer gives instant SmartScreen reputation. Fixes: (a) show those two clicks with a picture right after the Download click (part of Fix 2's web half); (b) submit the installer at Microsoft's file submission portal for reputation review (Majid's account); (c) someone at the laptop downloads the installer in Edge and records what Edge and Windows show, without installing.
- **The home page is sent uncompressed.** A first visit is ~805 KB in 24 files, 441 KB of it uncompressed text. The page (135 KB), `file-icon-vectors.css` (170 KB) and `bootstrap-icons.min.css` (84 KB) block the first screen. Response compression (Brotli/gzip) cuts them to about a quarter: a few lines in `Program.cs`, staging first.

## About the budget

Majid allowed any campaign cost. The recommendation is still **not to raise budgets until the first real sign-ups come through**. Of ~33 real ad visitors last night, 0 reached the sign-up form, and 3 in 4 downloaders never open the app. More budget now buys more of the same leak. Raise once Fixes 1-3 are live and a sign-up has been counted.

## 3. What not to do yet

- **Don't raise any budget.** Conversions are 0 and may not even be counted. More spend buys more of the same leak.
- **Don't make "account created" or "installer download" a Primary conversion** without Majid. Google would learn to buy people who never confirm.
- **Don't add a Meta pixel** until Majid approves the banner and privacy wording that names Meta.
- **Don't switch to "advanced" consent mode.** It reverses Majid's earlier decision.
- **Don't trust GA4 or the "77 ad visits" number.**
  - GA4 only sees people who accept cookies.
  - The 77 counts tagged page loads, not ad clicks. Google Ads shows 37 clicks.
- **Don't make paid plans lock features** (AI connector, upload). That breaks the free Android app and the reviewer account for the Claude and ChatGPT directories. The pricing model is Majid's decision for later.
- **Don't turn on Google sign-in as it is.** Accounts would have no password, so they could not sign in on the PC app. The email is typed freely, and two-factor is skipped.
- **Don't make the server say "no account with this email".** It lets anyone check which emails are customers. Worse, the app saves any unknown reply as its device token.
- **Don't swap the captcha to Turnstile yet.** Turnstile lets everyone through when its key is missing.
- **Don't run the test purchase on Majid's account.** It is reset to the top plan at every restart, and the purchase would lock some of his PCs.
- **Don't republish the installer under the 1.2.8.1 name.** It would overwrite the live file, and the checksum shown on /Download would stop matching.
- **Don't move the Windows download constants without the auto-update settings.** The whole fleet would download files that fail the check.
- **Don't open Paddle checkout automatically on page load.**
- **Don't write "nothing is uploaded", "never stored" or "never copied" in any copy.** The storage wording test rejects them, and they are not true.
- **The malformed-email fix is already Finch's.** Not repeated here.
