BRAWL LEGENDS

Pick your beast. Rule the arena.

How to Play

  • Move — WASD or arrow keys (drag left side on mobile)
  • Aim & Shoot — move the mouse and hold left click (drag right side on mobile)
  • SUPER — press Space or Q when the bar is full (tap SUPER button)
  • Hide in bushes 🌳 to go invisible. Break crates 📦 for power-ups.
  • Defeat enemy brawlers to charge your SUPER and rack up your score.
