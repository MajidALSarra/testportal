Pick your beast. Rule the arena.

How to Play

  • Move — WASD or arrow keys (drag the LEFT half of the screen on mobile)
  • Aim & Shoot — move the mouse and hold left click (drag the RIGHT half of the screen on mobile)
  • SUPER — press Space or Q when the bar is full (tap the SUPER button)
  • Hide in bushes 🌳 to go invisible. Break crates 📦 for power-ups.
  • 1v1 duel — first to win 2 rounds takes the match. Deal damage to charge your SUPER.
